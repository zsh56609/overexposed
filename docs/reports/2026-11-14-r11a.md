## What was run

- **Script:** `node sim/survey/survey.ts` (flags: `--sequences=2000 --seed=20261114 --workers=<n> --out=sim/survey/results`). Files: `sim/survey/survey.ts` (the play, the counts, the workers), `inventory.ts`, `inventory-tables.ts`, `tables.ts`. It imports `/core`, `/sim` (`runOne`, the personas) and `/validate`; nothing else imports it.
- **Cells [S]:** 9 personas (`minmaxer`, `random`, `crafter`, `hypechaser`, `dealseeker`, `comeback`, `artisan`, `screenseeker`, `celebseeker`) + `mixed`, × 2 managers (`veteran` = Dex Holloway, `guardian` = Marguerite "Mags" Ashby) = 20 cells × **2,000 five-year sequences** = 200,000 runs, 107 s on 15 workers. `crafter`, `hypechaser`, `screenseeker`, `celebseeker` are the sim's probes, not player models; they are in the per-cell tables, not in the mixed player.
- **Seeds:** sequence *i* of every cell runs on `deriveSeed(20261114, i)`; its year *y* on `deriveSeed(sequenceSeed, y)`; the mixed player picks its strategy for year *y* from a seeded cursor on `deriveSeed(sequenceSeed, 0x6d1c)`. All cells see the same deals.
- **Determinism:** counts are integers, merged in a fixed order, keys sorted on output; two full runs wrote byte-identical `survey.json` and `tables.md` (`cmp`; three full runs compared, the last two after the final edit).
- **The memory.** It lives in two places. The core half is in the simulation already: `createInitialState(seed, content, { memory })` → `core/memory.ts` (`memoryFor`, `settleLines`) and `core/events.ts` (`eventCandidates` leaves out the events the last run met while anything else is eligible; it never holds back a follow-up or a `first`-marked one-off). `sim/batch.ts` `runOne` already takes `options.memory`. The storage half lives only in the UI: `ui/memory.ts` keeps *the events offered in the latest earlier run that met any* and, for each once-a-run group, *the variant last shown*; `ui/App.tsx` writes the lines when they are shown (opening, each season's card after the first, the doors offered, the ending). I reproduced that bookkeeping in `nextMemory()` in the survey script, from reading the source; I did not move or change the original. The memory persists across years exactly as the UI does: events of the last year that met any; the last variant shown of each once-a-run group, kept until that group is shown again.
- **New player vs returning.** Year 1 passes `skipFirstDraft: true` (as `ui/App.tsx` `makeRun` does when notes are on); years 2–5 are plain runs from the memory. The manager's notes themselves are UI-only and play no part in the simulation (see "Not measured").
- **What counts as "shown".** Cards offered = every card on a `draftOffer` (rerolls included); played, drafted = `RunRecord.plays/drafted`; events = every `eventOffered`; scandal cards = those crystallised; doors = those offered; endings and awards = the year's minor/major ending and `yearAwards`. Variant texts are read as `sim/variants.ts` and the UI read them: the player's printed headlines and scandal in-hand lines (by walking `LineCounter`), event answers, the front pages' items (`frontPages`), the manager's messages, sign-offs and month-end lines, and the once-a-run lines. **Newspaper items are counted twice:** across every paper (as `sim:variants` does: "every paper is readable") and for the lead paper only, the one on the desk (`MonthPress.lead`). Which is nearer to what a player reads is the author's call; the second is the lower bound.

## B. Five years in a row [S]

### B1. Repeat and coverage (T1, T2 above; by cell, T3)
- **Events.** The memory works: only 3.0% of year 2's events were in year 1 (those are chain follow-ups and the rival's `first`-marked text, which the memory never holds back; it also falls back when nothing else is eligible: 2.2% of month-6 slots). The repeat rate then rises to 48.2% in year 3 and 62.3% in year 4. A five-year player has met 18.8 of 28 events (67.1%); the mixed player sees each event in 2.7%–42% of years (top: `producer_room`, `sam_rent`, `mum_sunday` 42%; bottom: `sam_payback` 2.7%, `sam_band` 3.2%, `director_read` 14%).
- **Strategy matters more than manager for events.** Distinct events after 5 years: `crafter` 11.9, `artisan` 14.9, every other persona 18.0–18.9, `random`/`mixed` 18.8–18.9; events per year `crafter` 5.5 (a month with nothing eligible), everyone else 5.9–6.0. - **Cards offered depend on the manager.** Dex (`veteran`, +1 offered card) shows 23.3 distinct cards a year and repeats 62.9% in year 2; Mags (`guardian`) 17.9 and 47.3% (mixed player). Both reach 93%+ by year 5; Dex over 50% in year 2, Mags in year 3 (except `random`, year 2).
- **Once-a-run texts:** year 2 repeats **0.0%** in every cell (the memory), then 92–98% from year 3, because every group has two variants; coverage after 5 years is 52.6–55.5% in every cell (52 variants, 13 shown a year, 26 groups).
- **Newspapers:** with every paper counted, 90% of year 2's items were seen in year 1 (118 items a year from a pool of 165, with the sagas and the rival's arc printed every year); the lead paper alone repeats 32.6% in year 2 and passes 50% in year 3.

## Not measured, and why

- **The manager's notes** (UI only: `ui/notes.ts`, switched on by House rules, tied to the browser's storage). Counted from files only (A5). Not in the five years: the simulation does not play the UI.
- **A person.** The strategies are the sim's personas; "mixed" is a random draw among five of them. A real player's year-to-year behaviour (and which of 165 newspaper items they actually read) is not in these numbers.
- **The browser's storage.** `ui/memory.ts` was re-read and reproduced, not run; a player who clears storage, uses a private window or replays with `?seed=` has no memory (the UI says so in the file's header).
- **Reasons an event is out** are classified by the survey script, in one fixed order (already met, month window, rival arc, condition, chain not open, chains full, a follow-up/first took the slot, memory). The pool itself is `core/events.ts` `eventCandidates`, called on the real state at each event month.
- **Which of two papers the player reads,** see "What counts as shown".
- **Story-text quality or how repetitive a variant feels** is out of scope.
