New minor ending (The Long Game, celebrity lane)

the_one_to_watch — The One to Watch
You're not famous. Not yet. But your name keeps turning up on the lists — the ones to watch, the ones about to break — and people you've never met already have opinions about you. You spent the year being seen everywhere that matters. Next year you find out whether being seen is the same thing as being known.
