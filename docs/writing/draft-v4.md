New minor endings (the four existing minors keep their text)

the_independent — The Independent
You did it without them. No label, no machine, no one telling you which songs to cut. The numbers are yours and so is every decision behind them, including the ones that kept you up at night. It's lonelier than the other way. The industry still isn't sure what to call you. You've stopped needing them to.

leading_role — Leading Role
It started as a cameo. Now your name is above the title. People who never heard you sing know your face from the screen, and some of them are surprised to learn you ever did anything else. You still hum between takes. The camera turned out to be another kind of audience — one that remembers everything, and forgives more than you expected.

household_name — Household Name
You're not just a singer anymore. You're a face on a billboard, a guest on every show, a name people use as shorthand. Strangers feel like they know you. In a way, they do — you've given them so much of yourself that there's a version of you everywhere. The hard part now is remembering which one is real.

redemption_arc — The Redemption Arc
For a while there, it looked like the story was going to be the scandal. It nearly was. But you stopped running, answered for what you could, and did the work in public where everyone could watch you fail again. They didn't get the ending they were writing. Comebacks are the only story the papers love more than a fall.

tabloid_royalty — Tabloid Royalty
Somewhere along the way you stopped fighting the headlines and started writing them. Every feud is a trending topic, every rumour a week of free publicity. You're the name editors reach for on a slow day. It works, in its way. It just means you can never go quiet again — because the moment you do, they'll find someone else to be you.

character_actor — The Character Actor
You're not the star. You're the one directors call when the scene has to work. Casting sheets say "the singer from that thing" and then, a few years later, just your name. It isn't fame. It's steadier than fame — the kind of career where you're always working, and nobody ever asks you to be anyone but good.

flash_in_the_pan — Flash in the Pan
For one month, everyone knew your name. You remember the exact week it peaked — the week you checked your phone forty times a day. Then it moved on, the way it always does, and took the attention with it. You're left with the memory, a few good stories, and the strange knowledge of exactly how fast it can all go.

running_on_empty — Running on Empty
You gave it everything and it took everything. The shows got harder to finish, the songs harder to find, and somewhere in the autumn you stopped remembering why you started. You're tired in a way sleep doesn't fix. This isn't the end. But the next thing you do has to be rest, or there won't be a next thing.

starting_over — Starting Over
It didn't go the way you planned. You took the chances that looked like shortcuts and found out most of them weren't. But you're still here, which is more than most. You know things now that you didn't in the spring — about the business, about the press, about yourself. That's not nothing. Next year, you'll know where the traps are.

Running on Empty repeats the Burnout card's in-hand line on purpose:
the line the player carried all year is where they ended up.
