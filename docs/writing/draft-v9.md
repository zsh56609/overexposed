Provenance: Drafted with AI assistance and accepted by the author.
Round R2: the events — fifteen, in three chains and six one-offs.

Conventions. A phone event shows a caption: the kicker
"<Month Year> · <category>", the event's title, and its category's
line. A letter has no caption: its letterhead and date line are its
heading. Replies are the player's words; outcomes are the other side's
reply (a phone) or the result (a letter, in italic after the SENT
stamp). Effects are written as on the card faces; "−1 action" is an
action spent this month; "Tabloid Story" and "Public Feud" are the
existing scandal cards, added to the discard pile.

Category lines
- personal: Not everything this year happens on a stage.
- industry: Someone in the business has noticed you.
- press: They're writing about you. They'd like a word first.
- rival: Your rival, being friendly. Probably.
