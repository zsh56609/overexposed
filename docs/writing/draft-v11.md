Provenance: Drafted with AI assistance and accepted by the author.
Round R8b: the manager's notes for a new player's first year, the House
rules switch that turns them on and off, and the credits' typefaces row
laid out again (its words moved, not new). Approved 2026-10-20.

Conventions. A note is in its manager's hand: Dex (veteran) and Mags
(guardian) each write all six, signed. The marks [hype], [craft] and
[heat] are drawn as the stat bar's icons, not printed. Game terms are
capitalised as the cards are (Hype, Craft, Heat, Scandal, Lay Low, Crisis
PR). The actions and mirror notes have a .touch line, used when the last
pointer put down was touch or a pen: "hold" where a mouse would hover.
