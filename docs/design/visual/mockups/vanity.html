The press study. Three papers stand on the desk; the one that printed your biggest story that month is in front. How much of it is about you depends on how famous you are — unknown, you are a line in the corner; famous, you fill the page; in a frenzy, you are in every paper. The rest is the world: other people, the weather, and a rival named Juno Vale. Your private practice goes in the notebook, not the press. Placeholder numbers.
